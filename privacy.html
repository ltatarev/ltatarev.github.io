The short version

  • There's no account, so there's nothing to sign up with and nothing to leak.
  • Your watchlist, ratings, reviews and lists are stored on your device only.
  • The app asks a catalogue server for film and show data. Those requests carry a title ID, not who you are.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. There are no ads.
  • Delete the app and the data goes with it.

What's on your device

Everything you create in Moviest is written to a database on your phone and stays there. That includes:

  • Your watchlist, watched history and per-episode progress
  • Ratings, reviews and any lists you make
  • App settings — region, streaming services you subscribe to, appearance
  • A cache of artwork and title details so screens open without a connection

None of it is uploaded. It is included in your device's own backup (iCloud or an encrypted local backup) if you have backups turned on, because it's part of the app's storage — that backup is governed by Apple's terms, not ours.

What leaves your device

Three things, and they're worth being precise about.

Catalogue requests

To show you a poster, a cast list or a return date, the app requests it from our proxy, which passes it to TMDB. Each request carries the title or person being looked up and your chosen region. It does not carry your name, an account, an advertising identifier or your library. Our proxy keeps standard short-lived server logs — IP address, timestamp, path — used to keep the service running and stop abuse, and kept for N days before deletion.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Contacts, photos, location, calendar or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking
  • Anything sold or licensed to a data broker — this has never happened and is not a business we're in

Imports

If you import an IMDb export, the CSV file is read on your device and matched against the catalogue there. The file is not uploaded, and only the titles you confirm are written into your library. Matching a title uses the same catalogue request as any other lookup.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, most data-protection requests have a short answer: there's nothing on our side to access, correct or export. You can act on everything yourself:

  • See it — it's all in the app, on the Library and You tabs
  • Change it — every rating, review and list is editable
  • Delete it — clear the library in Settings, or delete the app

If you're in the EU or UK and want to make a request about the proxy's server logs, write to us at the address below. The data controller is [legal entity or your name, and postal address].

Children

Moviest isn't directed at children under 13 / 16 — match your App Store age rating and we don't knowingly collect anything from them. Since the app collects no personal data from anyone, there's nothing on file to remove.
